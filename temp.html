6 · How it behaves

Header

  • Hidden at the very top of the page.
  • Slides down (about 200ms) as soon as the page scrolls.
  • Slides away again when the visitor scrolls back to the top.
  • Keyboard users get it back the moment focus moves past the cover; reduced motion shows and hides it without the slide.

The song (Spotify)

  • "Play …" swaps in Spotify's compact player in the same spot and starts it.
  • Visitors not logged in to Spotify hear a 30-second preview; logged-in visitors hear the whole track.
  • If a browser (iOS Safari) won't start it from our link, the visitor taps the player, which is now right there.
  • While it plays, the header's pill shows it and pauses it from anywhere on the page.
  • Nothing plays until the visitor asks; the player loads only then.

Photo and the rest of the page

  • The About photo, cropped to 3:4 around you and the husky.
  • The About section shows the same photo today. Suggest dropping it there so it isn't shown twice.
  • Below the cover: the intro band with the live pipeline graph, then About, Experience, Projects, Skills and Contact, unchanged.
  • Entrance: the name rises, then the play link and photo follow, once on load.
